If you contact me by phone, email or WhatsApp, I use your details exclusively to handle your enquiry and, where applicable, to prepare and carry out an order. This may involve processing your name, contact details, messages and voluntarily provided photographs.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the processing concerns steps before entering into a contract or performance of a contract. For general enquiries, processing may be based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR where applicable.
