06 Questions and answers

Common questions

How much would my job cost?+

Tell me what needs doing and send a few photos. I will ask about the details and let you know whether that is enough for an estimate or whether I should see the space first. We will discuss the price before work starts.

It is only a small job. Can I still get in touch?+

Yes, tell me what needs fixing or fitting. We can discuss small jobs too, and I will let you know whether I can help.

When could you start?+

Tell me what needs doing and when would suit you. I will check my schedule, then we can agree on a date. You are welcome to ask about urgent jobs too.

Can I just send you photos on WhatsApp?+

Yes, that is a good way to start. Send a few photos and tell me what you would like to change. If something is not clear, I will ask a question or request another photo.

More questionsFewer questions
Do I buy the materials, or can you help?+

If you already have materials, tell me what you have. If you have not bought anything yet, we will first discuss what is needed and who will buy it. That helps avoid buying too much or choosing unsuitable materials.

What if I do not know exactly what I want yet?+

You do not need a finished plan. Tell me what is bothering you and what you would like to improve. We can discuss the options and where to start.

How long will the work take?+

That depends on the amount of work, the condition of the space and the materials. Once we have discussed the details, we will go over the expected time, including any drying time needed.

Can I stay in the flat while the work is being done?+

That depends on the work and which rooms are affected. We will talk about dust, noise and access beforehand so you can plan your day-to-day routine.

What should I prepare before you come?+

To start with, a short description, some photos and the location are enough. Once we agree on the work, I will explain where I need clear access and what should be moved or covered.

Do you travel outside Frankfurt?+

I work in Frankfurt am Main and the surrounding area. Send me the town or postcode, and we will check whether I can come and discuss any travel arrangements.

What if you find another problem during the work?+

If extra work is needed, we will discuss it first, including how it affects the price and timing. We will agree on the extra work before it is carried out.

How can I pay?+

We will agree on the payment method and timing before work starts. If you need an invoice or have a particular payment request, please mention it when we first speak.
